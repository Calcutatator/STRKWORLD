opaque and privacy-neutral.

Direction notes from approval:

- Keep all eight directions.
- Make hub-world versions distinctly cosy, casual and approachable.
- Bring the final polish closer to warm late-16-bit top-down JRPG sprite craft,
  while staying original and non-derivative.
- Characters 4 and 7 are intentionally larger.
- Character 6 is intentionally smaller, chibi-esque, cute, and female.
- The fighting state is a visual stance only, not gameplay or privacy state.
